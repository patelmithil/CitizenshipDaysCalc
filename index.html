B
Temporary Resident / Protected Person Status
Did you have Canadian temporary resident status or were you a protected person within the 5-year window before becoming a PR?
Each day physically present in Canada as a temporary resident or protected person before PR counts as ½ day, up to a maximum credit of 365 days.
⚠️ Important: Work/study permits held while a refugee claim or PRRA was being assessed do not grant temporary resident status. Only time after a positive Protected Person decision counts.
C
Time Serving a Sentence
Did you spend time serving a sentence for an offence in Canada (imprisonment, conditional sentence, probation, parole)?
⚠️ Time spent serving a sentence cannot be counted towards physical presence.
D
Absences from Canada
List all time spent outside Canada in the 5-year window, including work travel and vacations.
📌 How days are counted
  • Any part of a day physically in Canada = 1 full day.
  • The day you leave counts as a day in Canada.
  • The day you return also counts as a day in Canada.
Only full days between departure and return (exclusive of both) are absent days.
